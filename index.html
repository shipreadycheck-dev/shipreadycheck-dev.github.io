The mistakes AI tools make most, checked by name

AI writes code that works on the happy path. The dangerous parts are invisible until a stranger finds them. These are the ones behind the real leaks.

Secret keys in your website

OpenAI, Stripe and database keys that anyone can copy from the browser.

Database open to the public

Supabase tables with Row Level Security off, or rules that let everyone in. Firebase left in test mode.

Users seeing each other's data

Pages and API routes that don't check the record belongs to who's asking.

Free upgrades

Unverified Stripe webhooks, prices set in the browser, success pages that unlock access.

Surprise bills

AI endpoints with no login or rate limit, which one bot can loop all night on your card.

Risky leftovers

Debug mode on, wide-open CORS, committed .env files, look-alike packages.

    Questions

    I'm not a programmer. Will I understand the report?

    Yes. Every issue says what's wrong, why it matters in plain English, and what to do. The Full Check gives you prompts to paste straight into the tool you built with.

    Is it safe to send my code?

    Send a public repo link or a .zip, never passwords or live keys. We only read your code; we never run it. It's deleted once your report is delivered. If we find an exposed key, the report tells you to rotate it first.

    My repo is private.

    Download it as a .zip (on GitHub: Code, then Download ZIP) and send that instead.

    What stacks do you cover?

    Best coverage: Next.js, React, Vite, Supabase, Firebase, Stripe, and apps calling OpenAI, Anthropic or Gemini. Other stacks get the general checks; we'll say clearly what we couldn't check.

    What if you find nothing?

    Then you launch with more confidence, and the report shows exactly what was checked.

    Is there a free version?

    Yes. Our basic scanner is free and open source on GitHub, if you're comfortable running it yourself. The paid check adds a manual review of what patterns can't see, a plain-English report, and fix prompts.

    Refunds?

    If we can't deliver your report within 24 hours, or can't check your project at all, you get a full refund.
